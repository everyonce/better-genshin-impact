# Field recognition and bounded diagnostic status

The exact-level recognizer remains fail-closed. A larger pale-label mask fixes a clipped baseline, and one bounded tight crop recovers some distant labels. Neither change makes distant level recognition reliable enough for repeated combat attempts.

Actual in-process OCR replay completed 133 of 134 retained cases; one historical image had incompatible crop dimensions. The current full detector accepted 11 of 25 positive candidates (44%) and accepted none of 101 inventory/no-red controls. Seven separate player-level-16 crops produced no level-4 matches. Narrower crop variants reached at most 11/25 individually and 14/25 in union. Label-only matches on four no-red controls demonstrate why the independent red-bar requirement must remain. No token substring relaxation or crop experiment was promoted.

The opt-in single-target diagnostic allows bounded camera alignment and up to four 400 ms approaches, preserving the existing close-range criterion. It permits at most three normal attacks with fresh identity, range and health checks between strikes, then returns to the existing verified-pause guardian. Identity ambiguity, any observed in-batch health loss, range loss or deadline ends the attempt. Red-only contours lack a full-track denominator, so the diagnostic records observations without claiming damage or defeat.

Validation: the workspace JavaScript suite passed 1,413 tests; 61 focused publication tests passed after converting the new diagnostic fixture to a synthetic account identity. Live attempts exercised recognition refusal and verified pause only. Approach, strike sequencing and the corrected exact-level bar schema are unit-tested; successful live combat and damage measurement remain unvalidated. Native route telemetry and journal guards included in this change have their own bounded checks and tests.

Private screenshots, account/session evidence, runtime probe hooks and experimental crop variants are excluded from publication. The target experiment is parked; further progress should select a concrete noncombat Adventure EXP reward before resuming travel.
