# Shield response scope

The local policy separates material shields from elemental barriers, uses only fresh tracked targets and verified available party capabilities, and refuses unready, out-of-range, interrupted or unsafe actions. At most two attempts are eligible; an established ineffective application stops an unchanged retry. Application is not removal, and disappearing targets or damage numbers do not establish a shield break.

Planning rules use [KQM wooden Shieldwall data](https://library.keqingmains.com/enemy-data/hilichurls/mitachurls/wooden-shieldwall-mitachurl) and [Abyss Mage data](https://library.keqingmains.com/enemy-data/abyss-order/abyss-mage). Wood receives Pyro. Among the current starter party, Cryo barriers favor Pyro, Hydro barriers favor Cryo, Pyro barriers favor Electro, and Electro barriers permit Cryo or Pyro. This restricted table is not a complete ranking of every element. Elemental-barrier recognition and automatic shield-removal detection remain unsupported in the live adapter.

[Amber mechanics](https://library.keqingmains.com/characters/pyro/amber): a fully charged arrow applies Pyro; Baron Bunny applies Pyro when destroyed or after its timer, with an eight-second duration after landing; burst lasts two seconds and costs forty energy. The policy compares usable primitives by counter suitability and bounded exposure. A delayed decoy is unsuitable for the current short application window. Readiness, target range and facing must be checked independently.
